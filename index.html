Questions

ASK US ANYTHING.

Do I need to own my home?

No. Many foster carers rent. You need a stable home and a spare bedroom for a child.

Can I foster if I'm single?

Yes. Single people make wonderful foster carers. What matters is the care, time and stability you can offer.

Can I work and foster?

It depends on the type of fostering and your hours. Some placements need a carer at home full time; others fit around work. We'll talk it through honestly.

Is there an age limit?

There's no upper age limit. Most agencies ask that applicants are at least 21, and what matters is that you're healthy and able to meet a child's needs.

Do I get paid while I'm being assessed?

Fees and allowances start when a child is placed with you. Assessment and training are free.

I already foster. Can I move to Regent?

Yes. Transfers follow the national transfer protocol so children stay settled. Get in touch for a confidential chat.

How transferring works

A CAREFUL,
PLANNED MOVE.

Transfers follow the national transfer protocol for foster carers, which puts the children's stability first.

  1. 01Confidential chatTalk to us privately. Nothing is shared until you decide.
  2. 02Let your agency knowYou tell your current agency you'd like to move.
  3. 03Transfer meetingBoth agencies and the placing authority plan the move together.
  4. 04AssessmentWe update your assessment, building on what's already known.
  5. 05PanelYou're approved by Regent's fostering panel.
  6. 06Welcome homeYour approval moves over, with no gap in support.

Transfer questions

WHAT CARERS ASK US.

Will the children in my care have to move?

The aim of the transfer protocol is that children stay with you. The placing authority is involved throughout and agrees the plan.

Will my pay stop during the move?

No. We plan the move so there's no gap in your fees or allowances. We'll show you exactly what you'll receive with Regent before you decide.

How long does a transfer take?

It depends on your circumstances and the agencies involved. We'll give you a realistic timeline at the start and keep you updated.

Is the first conversation confidential?

Yes. We won't contact your current agency or anyone else until you've decided to go ahead.
